# Remaining-nine Forge AI behavioral expectations

These expectations were written before the 52-game validation outcomes were generated. They are derived from the unchanged deck lists at validated commit `29ac1c40fee5a7058f040398654198ae270f5b22` and the repository's Battle Box documentation. They are behavioral checklists, not balance targets.

## Tron

Deck core: Expedition Map, Ancient Stirrings, Crop Rotation, Chromatic Star/Sphere, Energy Refractor; Urza's Mine/Power Plant/Tower; Moment's Peace and life gain; Mulldrifter/Mnemonic Wall; Fangren Marauder, Ulamog's Crusher, Rolling Thunder, Steel Hellkite.

Observable expectations:
- develop mana and seek missing Mine/Tower/Power Plant pieces when a choice is visible;
- preserve a unique assembled Tron piece and scarce Forest/green source unless a contrary reason is visible;
- use Star/Sphere when colored mana is actually needed and the activation is explicit;
- spend assembled Tron mana legally on card advantage, stabilization, or payoffs;
- cast and use payoffs when reached;
- avoid clearly destructive land sacrifice/discard decisions that break assembled Tron without a demonstrated reason.

Focused opponents: White Weenie (aggro pressure), Blue Terror (stack interaction), Jund Wildfire (resource/midrange pressure).

## Esper Control

Deck core: Counterspell, Cast Down, Doom Blade, Journey to Nowhere, Drown in the Loch, Supreme Verdict, Prismatic Strands; Preordain/Lorien Revealed/Fact or Fiction/Deep Analysis; Mulldrifter/Archaeomancer/Ephemerate; Thorn of the Black Rose and Gurmag Angler.

Observable expectations:
- targeted removal uses legal, meaningful creature targets;
- Counterspell is used on a meaningful spell when the target is visible;
- Supreme Verdict is used in a board state where visible creature pressure supports sweeping;
- Prismatic Strands prevents substantial or lethal same-color combat damage when reached;
- interaction preservation versus expenditure is assessed only if alternatives are actually visible;
- card-advantage/recursion pieces and eventual win conditions are executed without loops or nonproductive repetition.

Focused opponents: White Weenie (creature aggression), Blue Terror (stack/tempo pressure), Black Sacrifice (sacrifice pressure).

## White Weenie

Low-curve artifact-synergy aggro with Ardent Recruit/Court Homunculus, evasive/recursive value through Kor Skyfisher and Battle Screech, pump through Ramosian Rally/Anafenza/Benalish Marshal, and Journey/Thraben Charm/Prismatic Strands interaction.

Checklist: deploy early creatures and Ancient Den mana; attack when profitable; use pump/combat protection coherently; use removal on meaningful targets; execute Battle Screech and artifact synergies; avoid repeated passes with material legal development visible.

Representative opponent: Jund Wildfire.

## Madness Burn

Fast red pressure using Swiftspear/Dragon's Rage Channeler, discard/draw engines Faithless Looting and Grab the Prize, madness Fiery Temper, and direct damage Lightning Bolt/Lava Dart/Fireblast/Skullcrack.

Checklist: develop threats; convert discard into madness/value when observable; direct burn at sensible creature/player targets; sequence prowess/noncreature spells coherently; use Fireblast/Lava Dart costs legally; avoid wasting burn on irrelevant targets when a superior visible target is established.

Representative opponent: Esper Control.

## Green Stompy

Creature-dense aggro using Nettle Sentinel, Quirion Ranger, Skarrgan Pit-Skulk, Young Wolf, Burning-Tree Emissary and Strangleroot Geist, backed by Rancor, Vines, Savage Swipe and Hunger of the Howlpack.

Checklist: curve creatures; exploit Burning-Tree mana when possible; attack aggressively but not suicidally; use Rancor/pump/fight effects on legal productive targets; use Vines as protection/pump coherently; execute undying/morbid synergies when reached.

Representative opponent: Esper Control.

## Black Sacrifice

Aristocrats/sacrifice aggro using Carrion Feeder, Bloodthrone Vampire, Ravenous Squirrel, Gixian Infiltrator, Nested Shambler and disposable bodies, with Village Rites/Corrupted Conviction, Accursed Marauder, Supernatural Stamina and Dark Triumph.

Checklist: establish sacrifice outlets and fodder; sacrifice creatures for actual value; grow payoff creatures; use draw-sacrifice spells on sensible fodder; attack/block consistent with sacrifice incentives; avoid consuming key payoff creatures for negligible benefit when an alternative is visibly available.

Representative opponent: White Weenie.

## Blue Terror

Tempo-control using Delver, Tolarian Terror/Cryptic Serpent, cantrips and Thought Scour to fuel graveyard-based cost reduction, with Counterspell, Daze, Foil, Lose Focus and Deem Inferior.

Checklist: develop mana/cantrips; fill graveyard; deploy discounted threats when reached; counter meaningful spells; use Deem Inferior on meaningful legal targets; attack with threats; avoid discarding/returning resources for free counters without a visible reason when alternatives are known.

Representative opponent: Jund Wildfire.

## Jund Wildfire

Artifact-resource midrange using indestructible bridges plus Cleansing Wildfire, Ichor Wellspring, Deadly Dispute/Eviscerator's Insight, Refurbished Familiar and sacrifice payoffs, with Cast Down, Writhing Chrysalis, Mayhem Devil, Crypt Rats and Makeshift Munitions.

Checklist: develop colors; use Wildfire on profitable/appropriate lands, especially own bridges when that line is visible; convert artifacts into cards/value; target removal sensibly; execute sacrifice triggers; cast and attack/block with midrange payoffs; avoid destructive mana-base targeting without visible compensation.

Representative opponent: Blue Terror.

## Sultai Beans

Graveyard/value midrange built around Up the Beanstalk with high-mana-value cards that can be discounted or landcycled: Generous Ent, Chitin Gravestalker, Gurmag Nightwatch, Nemesis of Mortals, plus Malevolent Rumble, Mire Triton and Overwhelming Remorse.

Checklist: develop/fix mana; cast Up the Beanstalk; fill graveyard; use landcycling/value setup coherently; deploy discounted high-MV threats; use Overwhelming Remorse/Far // Away on meaningful targets; exploit Beanstalk triggers when reached; avoid graveyard-resource consumption that visibly prevents a superior available payoff.

Representative opponent: White Weenie.

## Evidence policy

A completed game is not evidence that a checklist item works. Behaviors are classified only as `demonstrated correct`, `demonstrated defect`, `unobservable`, or `not reached`. A defect requires affirmative log evidence for both the selected action and any claimed legal alternative. Win/loss outcome is directional context only.
