### Substitution Policy

- Original cards from each archetype are preserved where Forge recognizes them.
- Only when Forge does not recognize a card do we substitute the closest functionally equivalent supported card.
- All substitutions are listed below with reason and affected deck.
- **This baseline is frozen at v1.0—no deck tuning after results are generated.**
